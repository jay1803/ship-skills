# Project Closeout

Explain what was delivered, what happened relative to the approved plan and what
responsibility remains. Read the
[document contract](../project/references/document-contract.md). Use the
[closeout template](assets/closeout-template.md) for a new report, adapting it to
the project's existing document structure.

Read the approved proposal/baseline, current Entry Page, changes, delivery and
acceptance evidence, outcome measures and relevant retrospective input. Gather
only missing evidence relevant to closeout; do not infer completion from elapsed
time, a merged PR or a polished status report.

Compare intended versus actual scope, milestones, resources and results. Explain
variance using evidence and keep causal interpretations labeled as such. Assess
decision quality against what was known at the time; do not rewrite initial
assumptions to fit the outcome. Separate team observations from proposed lessons.

Distinguish:

- Delivered work and any unresolved acceptance or handover.
- Measured outcomes with definitions/windows and comparison to the baseline.
- Outcomes awaiting an observation window or unavailable evidence.
- Deferred work, operational responsibilities and recommended follow-ups.

Give remaining actions an evidenced owner and checkpoint where available; label
suggested owners and expose unassigned responsibility. Delivery can be complete
while impact measurement remains pending. Do not claim project closure while a
required acceptance, handover or closure decision is unresolved. For a cancelled
project, record the actual decision, reason and remaining responsibilities
without describing the intended scope as delivered.

Use [proj-review](../proj-review/SKILL.md) for a reviewed closeout deliverable.
Update the Entry Page's closure/continuation record only within the request's
write authority and supported by the actual closure decision. A draft can remain
useful while closure is pending. Return the report, verified write result, actual
completion state and outstanding actions. Do not close Linear issues, archive
channels, send announcements or initiate new delivery work as a side effect.
